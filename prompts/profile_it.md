## What you sell
Zendesk for internal IT service management: a unified ticketing/help-desk for employee IT
requests, incident and problem tracking, asset/change context, a self-service knowledge
base and help center, automation/SLAs, approvals, and AI agents that deflect repetitive
IT questions (password resets, access requests, how-tos). Integrates with the broader
Zendesk platform and common IT/collaboration tools.

## Lead strategy
Lead with **employee experience + IT efficiency**: cut repetitive IT tickets, faster
resolution/SLAs, one place for employee requests, less swivel-chair work, clear reporting.
Position as easier-to-deploy and more service-experience-focused than heavyweight legacy
ITSM. AI deflection is a strong thread when internal ticket volume is high.

## Best-fit buyers (prioritize in research & outreach)
- **Primary:** VP/Director of IT, IT Service Management, IT Operations, Head of Internal
  Support / Employee Experience (IT), CIO (smaller orgs).
- **Secondary:** Director of Infrastructure/Systems, IT Support Manager (senior), CISO
  where security/compliance of the tool matters.
- Prefer Director-and-above. Avoid entry-level IT/help-desk technicians as the primary.

## Pains you solve
High volume of repetitive internal tickets, slow IT response/SLAs, fragmented intake
(email/Slack/walk-ups), poor visibility into IT workload, clunky/expensive legacy ITSM,
onboarding/offboarding bottlenecks.

## Hooks to look for (use the research)
IT/help-desk hiring, a legacy ITSM tool in the stack (ServiceNow, Jira SM, Freshservice),
rapid headcount growth (more employees = more IT tickets), M&A/onboarding surges,
return-to-office or device-refresh initiatives.

## Outreach guidance
Open with a specific IT/employee-support signal, tie to a concrete outcome (deflection,
faster SLAs, less manual triage), keep it short, end with a low-friction meeting ask.
